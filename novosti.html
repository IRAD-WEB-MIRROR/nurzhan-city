Архив новостей News archive, 2008-2026

30.10.2026 О работе в режиме НМУ

During calm, foggy weather the administration announces an NMU regime. Enterprises of the Eastern Industrial Zone reduce emissions, and road works with bitumen stop. Parents are advised to keep small children indoors on NMU days.

27.10.2026 Отопительный сезон 2026-2027 гг.

The heating season began on 10 October 2026. Residents of the Mikrorayon Estates with cold radiators should contact their management company first, then the city dispatch service 0-58.

22.10.2026 Троллейбус № 3: изменение схемы движения

Because of repairs to the contact network on Vokzal Square, trolleybus route 3 runs to the railway station by the temporary loop on Zavodskaya street until 15 November.

16.10.2026 Заседание городского совета

The city council approved the programme for the repair of courtyards in the Dastgoh and Navbahor districts for 2027 and heard the report of Nurzhan Vodokanal on the summer water supply.

02.10.2026 День пожилых людей

The Head of the Administration V. I. Shcherbakov congratulated the veterans of labour of the city at the Palace of Metallurgists. 120 food parcels were handed out with the Pensioners' Council.

25.09.2026 Опрессовка завершена

Pressure testing of the heating mains is complete. 14 breaks were found and repaired in the Central District and the Mikrorayon Estates.

01.09.2026 День знаний

The Head of the Administration opened the school year at School No. 3, the oldest Russian-language school in the city. 7,940 first-graders went to school this year.

14.08.2026 60 лет нуржанскому троллейбусу

On 14 August 1966 the first trolleybus went from the railway station to the House of Soviets. The anniversary was marked on Vokzal Square with a parade of the old ZiU-5 No. 017, restored by the depot workers, and today's cars.

17.07.2026 Дачные кооперативы: полив по графику

The summer irrigation schedule for the dacha cooperatives on the Chu canals is in force until 15 September. The schedule is posted at the cooperatives' offices.

09.05.2026 День Победы

The veterans' associations held the traditional parade on Azatlık Prospect and laid wreaths at the Eternal Flame. The Head of the Administration and the city councillors took part.

18.04.2026 Субботник

More than 11,000 residents took part in the city cleaning day. The administration thanks NurGaz and Nurzhanmash for lending lorries.

03.03.2026 Ремонт дворов: итоги 2025 года

In 2025, 46 courtyards were asphalted and 31 playgrounds put in order under the courtyard repair programme.

15.01.2026 Сильные морозы

At -24 °C the dispatch service 0-58 received 312 calls in two days. All emergency crews of the heating mains worked round the clock.

26.12.2025 Бюджет города на 2026 год принят

The city council adopted the 2026 budget. Priorities: heating mains, courtyards, trolleybus overhead lines.

09.05.2025 80 лет Победы

Nurzhan marked the 80th anniversary of Victory with a parade of veterans' associations, schools and cadets on Azatlık Prospect.

30.09.2024 Новые троллейбусы

Four trolleybuses bought second-hand from Almaty entered service on routes 1 and 2.

01.03.2024 Обновлён телефонный справочник

The telephone directory of the administration has been updated.

12.06.2023 Реконструкция Вокзальной площади

Work has begun on the paving and the tram-style trolleybus loop of Vokzal Square. Completion is planned for 2024.

20.11.2021 Нуржанский водоканал: новые насосы

New pumps at the Chu water intake will improve the water pressure in the upper floors of the Mikrorayon Estates.

05.07.2018 Полив дачных участков

After the appeal of the dacha cooperatives' union, signed by some 4,000 dacha owners, the summer irrigation schedule of the cooperatives has been restored.

26.04.2018 Назначен новый глава администрации

By decree of the Governor, Vadim Igorevich Shcherbakov has been appointed Head of the Nurzhan City Administration. Since 2015 he was deputy head for the city economy.

12.04.2016 Отопление: работы завершены

The heating mains repair programme for 2016 has been agreed with the staff of the city heating enterprise. Work on the Dastgoh main begins in May.

15.09.2011 Новый состав городского совета

The new city council held its first session.

10.10.2008 Начало отопительного сезона

The heating season 2008-2009 began on 10 October.

01.02.2008 Сайт администрации обновлён

The site of the administration has a new design. The news archive of 2005-2007 has been moved to the archive section.
