Транспорт City transport

Троллейбус на маршруте. A trolleybus on the line.

Nurzhan has four trolleybus routes, running since 1966, about 90 marshrutka routes and city buses. Fare: 2 som on all city routes. Pensioners travel free on trolleybuses with a pension card.

Маршрутные такси Marshrutkas

About 90 routes. Main terminuses: Vokzal Square, Dastgoh market, Aeroport. Route maps are posted at the stops. Intercity marshrutkas and buses to Talas leave from the bus station on Vokzal Square.

Жалобы на работу общественного транспорта: отдел транспорта, каб. 214, тел. 2-40-11-44. Complaints about public transport: transport department, room 214.
