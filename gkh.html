Режим НМУ Unfavourable meteorological conditions

In winter, smoke from the refinery and the coal-fired heat plants is trapped under temperature inversions over the Chu plain. When the air quality index is above 100 the administration announces an NMU regime: enterprises reduce emissions, open burning is forbidden, and schools cancel outdoor PE. Readings come from the Talas State University air post in Dastgoh. The current status is on the right of this page.

Вывоз мусора. Rubbish is collected from the courtyard container sites daily except Sunday. Bulky waste: call your management company. Fine for burning leaves: 20 som.
